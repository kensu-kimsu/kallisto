# Portfolio Rules

## Microcap Growth

- U.S.-listed equities only
- Major U.S. exchanges
- Market capitalization below $300 million
- Approximately top 50 qualifying names by volume
- Whole shares only
- Long only
- LIMIT DAY orders
- Stop loss required for buys

## Nasdaq Titan Growth

- Approximately top 20 distinct Nasdaq-listed equities by market capitalization
- Nasdaq exchanges only
- Duplicate company share classes filtered where possible
- Whole shares only
- Long only
- LIMIT DAY orders

## Common Restrictions

- No short selling
- No margin
- No leverage
- No options
- No fractional shares
- No extended-hours execution
